# HYSTERIA

### A Tribute to Muse

---

*The riffs. The anthems. The atmosphere.*
*Brought back to life — every night.*

---

Hysteria is the UK's premier tribute to one of the greatest rock bands of the modern era. From the explosive energy of **Origin of Symmetry** to the orchestral grandeur of **The Resistance**, we deliver a live experience that captures everything that makes Muse iconic — the walls of guitar, the soaring vocals, the sheer scale of it all.

Whether you last saw Muse at a stadium or you've never caught them live, Hysteria brings that same rush to venues near you.
